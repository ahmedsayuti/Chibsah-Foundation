About Chibsah Foundation

Learn about our mission, our values, and the visionary behind our work to transform lives in Ghana through healthcare, clean water, and education.

Support Our Cause

Our Story

Chibsah Foundation was established in 2009 as a nonprofit subsidiary of YAC Group, born from a deep conviction that access to essential resources is a fundamental human right, not a privilege. Our journey began with a simple yet powerful vision: to create sustainable change in underserved communities across Ghana.

What started as modest community outreach initiatives has grown into a comprehensive organization impacting thousands of lives through healthcare programs, clean water projects, and educational support.

Today, we stand as a beacon of hope for countless families, working tirelessly to bridge the gap between need and opportunity. Our foundation operates on the principle that empowered communities create their own sustainable futures.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities in Ghana by providing essential healthcare, clean water, and educational resources, ensuring a brighter and healthier future for all. We strive to break the cycle of poverty through sustainable interventions that address root causes rather than symptoms.

Our Vision

A world where every individual, regardless of their background, has access to fundamental resources and opportunities for growth and development. We envision communities that are self-sufficient, educated, healthy, and equipped to create generational change.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every action we take

Compassion

We believe in serving with empathy and understanding, treating every individual with dignity and respect.

Sustainability

Our initiatives focus on long-term impact and self-sufficiency, creating solutions that last for generations.

Integrity

We uphold transparency and accountability in all our actions, ensuring donor funds are used effectively and ethically.

Community

We work with communities, not for them, ensuring local ownership and participation in all our projects.

Our Founder

The Visionary Behind Chibsah Foundation

Yussif Alhassan Chibsah

Founder & Chairman

From Football Legend to Humanitarian Leader

From the classrooms of Prempeh College to the stadium lights of Europe, Yussif Alhassan Chibsah's journey is one of discipline, resilience, and vision. Born in Accra in 1983, Yussif discovered early on that football was more than a game. It was a platform for leadership and service. His determination carried him from Ghana's local pitches to the international stage, where he became a respected midfielder for the Black Stars and captain of Asante Kotoko.

"Football gave me discipline, but education gave me purpose. I want to give both to the next generation."
— Yussif Alhassan Chibsah

A Career Across Continents

Yussif's talent opened doors far beyond Ghana. After excelling at home, he moved to Europe, where he played in Sweden's Allsvenskan league for clubs such as Gefle IF and Djurgårdens IF, making over 100 appearances. He also featured for GAIS and Ljungskile SK and had a spell in Türkiye with Alanyaspor. Each chapter of his career sharpened his skills, broadened his worldview, and deepened his belief that success must be paired with responsibility.

Education as Empowerment

Even at the height of his football career, Yussif never lost sight of education. After retiring from professional play, he pursued higher learning, earning a Master's degree in International Sports Law from ISDE Law Business School in Spain. For him, football taught discipline, but education unlocked purpose. This conviction fuels his mission to ensure that young people, especially those from disadvantaged backgrounds, have access to quality learning, mentorship, and opportunities to dream beyond their circumstances.

Club Consult Africa Ltd

Yussif is also the founder of Club Consult Africa Ltd, a sports management and consultancy firm dedicated to nurturing and guiding football talents across Africa. Through this platform, he provides professional representation, career development, and mentorship to young athletes, ensuring they are equipped not only for success on the pitch but also for life beyond football. His work with Club Consult Africa reflects his belief that talent must be matched with guidance, education, and opportunity to create lasting impact.

Our Impact

With the support of our partners, donors, and volunteers, Chibsah Foundation continues to expand its reach—ensuring that every person, regardless of their circumstances, has the opportunity to lead a healthier, safer, and more educated life.
